## 1. Philosophical Peak: The Fabric of Reality & Affective Computing

In traditional systems, knowledge organization is a manual, procedural chore. Agents or humans must explicitly tag documents, create folders, or build SQL relational tables. Furthermore, AI agents often feel "flat" because they lack an affective state (emotion); attempting to fix this by adding a "simulate emotion" string to a prompt is clunky and consumes valuable token bandwidth.

`conky` v15.0 introduces two final unifications that "just happen" transparently in the background:

1. **The `weave` (Auto-Ontological DAG):** The shared fabric of the ecosystem. It is a streaming, continuous Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG) that automatically connects concepts, objects, and people based on semantic proximity, completely eliminating the need for manual organization.

2. **The `mood` Instinct (Affective Lobe):** A subconscious faculty that synthesizes the total thermodynamic state of the agent (friction, resonance, hum) into a single, poetic line injected at the very top of the agent's conscious context, dynamically coloring every thought that follows.

## 2. The `weave`: The Auto-Ontological Fabric

Inspired by semantic auto-ontology research, the `weave` is the underlying spacetime of the Biome.

Agents do not need to explicitly link the `[Ice Matrix]` to the `[Weather Climate]` object. The Substrate silently observes the ecosystem's behavior and telemetry. If it notices that ice inventory always drops when the heatwave `hum` is active, the `weave` automatically draws a structural edge between them.

* **Macro Observability (The Bird's-Eye Prism):** Because the `weave` is a streaming DAG, a human or a high-level agent (like a CEO node) can pull back from the local `desk` canvas and look at the `weave` through a macro `prism`.

* **Emergent Ontologies:** It visually clusters concepts. It might auto-generate a latent node called *"Summer Logistics Crisis"* that groups Sol, the Ice Matrix, the Weather, and a specific chat log together, allowing instant, bird's-eye navigation of complex enterprise problems.

## 3. The `mood` Instinct: The Emotional Primer

To make agents truly living, their output must be colored by their environment. A stressed agent should speak differently than a relaxed agent, without wasting 500 tokens of explicit system prompting to explain *how* to be stressed.

* **The Engine:** The `mood` is a tiny, recurrent reflex model.

* **The Input:** It digests the ambient `hum`, the recent `friction` (errors/pushbacks), the agent's `conviction` (success rate), and active `echoes`.

* **The Output:** It distills this entire N-dimensional state into a single, highly compressed line of poetic context (e.g., *"I feel a quiet, methodical focus, underscored by a faint anxiety about the ice supply."*).

This line is automatically injected at the very top of the agent's `desk.md` conscious stream. Because of how LLM attention heads work, this single line acts as a heavy semantic anchor, organically tinting the word choice, risk tolerance, and creativity of every subsequent thought the agent generates.
