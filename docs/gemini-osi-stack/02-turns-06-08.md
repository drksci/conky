# 1. Product Requirements Document (PRD): The Living Mesh Runtime

## 1.1 Vision & Core Thesis

The Living Mesh is an Inversion-of-Control (IoC) agentic runtime where orchestration code is replaced by computational physics. Every actor—probabilistic LLM, deterministic rule engine, physical sensor, or human—is an autonomous cell owning its private state and projecting its boundary onto a federated GraphQL Mesh as living Markdown surface documents.

To the intelligence inside a cell, the harness does not exist. There are no system prompts lecturing the model about JSON schemas, step counters, or token budgets. The agent inhabits a Natural World of readable prose, live anchor gauges, physical port affordances, and inherited realm norms—while model tiering, context folding, causal persistence, and policy enforcement operate invisibly beneath the membrane like an autonomic nervous system.

## 1.2 Functional & Architectural Requirements

- **Zero-Orchestration Self-Assembly:** Bootstrapping a system requires instantiating a single seed cell inside a realm and injecting one goal signal. The cell must autonomously discover peer capabilities via beacon queries, negotiate schemas, and materialize living wire connectors.
- **Heterogeneous Engine Parity:** The mesh must treat probabilistic models (mind), deterministic WASM/state machines (gear), physical hardware/APIs (organ), and human interfaces (voice) identically at the transport boundary.
- **Ephemeral Living Surfaces:** All configuration, state, connectors, signals, and sagas exist as in-memory living Markdown documents (YAML frontmatter = deterministic physics; Markdown body = semantic reality). Nothing is persisted to disk unless crystallized as a causal moment.
- **Epistemic Transparency & Lazy Inheritance:** Ancestor policies and world state inherit lazily as collapsed @anchor pointers, unfolding into active context only when touched by the agent's attention and folding back once resolved.
- **Bi-Temporal Causal Replay:** Every state transition seals into a content-addressed moment stamped with a distributed flake clock, recording both true world-state and the exact perceived slice the agent saw—enabling lossless reminisce auditing and counterfactual relive simulation.

# 3. Multi-Axis Layered Architecture (From the Natural World Downward)

Every stratified subsystem in the runtime is structured into four concentric layers, ordered top-to-bottom from the Natural World perceived by the agent down to the invisible autonomic substrate.

## 3.1 The Reality & Execution Stack (Phenomenology to Compute)

**Layer R1 — The Natural World (Agent-Visible Horizon):**
What the agent actually sees: its own surface prose, live @anchor gauges inline in text, available port affordances, and inherited realm norms. Contains zero harness instructions or system boilerplate.

**Layer R2 — Attention & Framing (Epistemic Compiler):**
Intercepts @anchor references touched during reasoning, lazily executes GraphQL sub-queries to unfold relevant slices, packs KV-cache-aligned prefixes, and projects connected act ports as native tool affordances.

**Layer R3 — The Breath (Metabolic Actuation):**
Governs the cell's 4-beat execution step:

- `inhale`: Debounces incoming signal mutations over an attentional window (e.g., 200ms) sorted by flake.
- `attend`: Evaluates semantic complexity and triggers Layer R2 unfolding.
- `act`: Emits at most one coherent batch of frontmatter patches, ticket lease updates, or act port emissions.
- `exhale`: Seals the delta into a moment and returns the cell to zero-compute sleep.

**Layer R4 — Autonomic Substrate (Compute & Tiering):**
Dynamically routes attend/act phases to ultra-fast small models (or pure deterministic code) for routine reflex turns, seamlessly upshifting to frontier reasoning models when ward friction, rift harmonization, or dream planning occurs.

## 3.2 The Memory & Continuity Stack (Awareness to Archive)

**Layer M1 — Working Awareness (Active Horizon):**
The uncompressed surface frontmatter and immediate signal payloads active inside the current breath.

**Layer M2 — Episodic Wake (Causal Stream):**
The chain of moment nodes in `stream: waking` and isolated dream forks, each storing `caused_by` (flake triggers), `perceived` (the exact @anchor slice unfolded at that instant), and `delta`.

**Layer M3 — Consolidated Memory (Dreaming & Intuition):**
Triggered by quiet periods or a `pulse://sleep` metronome. Unremarkable sensor micro-moments evaporate; meaningful sequences weave into episode narratives (pinned by a blake3 Merkle root for lossless on-demand re-hydration) and distill recurring patterns into maxim priors.

**Layer M4 — Mnemonic Continuum (Cold Bi-Temporal Store):**
Content-addressed immutable storage backing `reminisce(at: flake)` (reconstructing exact past belief-state without hindsight leakage) and `relive(from: flake, mode: VERBATIM | UNPINNED)` (replaying history or testing counterfactual policy tweaks).

## 3.3 The Communication & Topology Stack (Meaning to MAC)

**Layer C1 — Semantic Dialogue (Prose & Sagas):**
Human- and LLM-legible Markdown bodies, inline @anchor transclusions, and shared ticket saga documents.

**Layer C2 — Impedance & Flow (wire Physics):**
Living wire surfaces mediating between ports. Enforces schema projection, delta-threshold backpressure (preventing noisy sensors from waking a mind cell), and pulse barrier synchronization.

**Layer C3 — Federated GraphQL Nervous System:**
A unified schema projected dynamically from all active surface frontmatter:

- `query`: Resolves `_discover(need: ...)` beacons and lazy @anchor paths.
- `mutation`: Applies atomic frontmatter patches or claims ticket leases.
- `subscription`: Drives event-driven wake-ups; idle cells consume zero compute.

**Layer C4 — Identity & Discovery (mac & beacon):**
Hardware-like mac addressing, flake logical clocks, and zero-config beacon gossip across local processes, containers, or wide-area networks.

## 3.4 The Governance & Agency Stack (Mandate to Arbitration)

**Layer G1 — Persona & Mandate (Teleology):**
The cell's identity, goals, and active maxim intuition written in plain Markdown prose.

**Layer G2 — Envelopes & Invariants (grant & ward):**
Declarative rules in frontmatter (`grant` allows; `ward` forbids). Evaluated deterministically by the wire membrane before any side effect leaves a port.

**Layer G3 — Dynamic Equilibrium (cascade & bubble):**
Mutating a parent realm triggers a cascade down the inheritance tree via GraphQL subscriptions. Hitting a ward boundary wraps the blocked intent and any supporting dream evidence into a bubble routed up to `escalate_to`.

**Layer G4 — Arbitration (rift Harmonization):**
When two nodes mutate coupled state concurrently (e.g., an agent raises price at the exact flake the owner lowers the cap), the substrate spawns a rift surface linking both moment nodes and wakes the designated arbiter cell to reconcile them.

## The Paradigm: Living Surfaces Breathing Over a Graph

Borrowing from Plan 9 (everything is a file) and Smalltalk (everything is a message), the runtime has only one data structure and one execution loop:

There is no orchestrator, no separate database, no ACL table, and no message broker. The GraphQL mesh simply projects the live graph of surfaces and resolves @anchor pointers on demand.

## 1. The Five Irreducible Primitives
