# 0002 Objective

## Decision

The objective is an autonomous organisation that can run, deliver and improve without its builders. An earlier agency project of the operator is the example of the objective, not a prescription for how to build it.

## Basis

Operator.

## Consequences

- Design choices are judged by whether they move toward an organisation that runs on its own.
- That earlier project may inform examples and tests but does not dictate mechanisms.
