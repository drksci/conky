# 0007 Plain vocabulary

## Decision

One plain word per concept: vault, page, key, link, embed, rule, event, seal, space, error; verbs read, write, watch. Earlier invented terms were dropped.

## Basis

Operator asked for words that resonate and are not camp. Architect chose them.

## Consequences

- A rename is atomic everywhere.
- A new concept gets a plain word or is not added.
