# 0012 Archive and rungs

## Decision

The Gemini conversation archive is source material only. An idea climbs one rung at a time: idea, folded, enforced, proven.

## Basis

Architect.

- idea: only in the archive or a note.
- folded: in docs with its law, what it composes from, what it removes, and the test that would falsify it.
- enforced: code plus a test that bites.
- proven: measured in an unscripted run.

## Consequences

- Every idea page states its rung.
- Nothing in the archive is a requirement.
