# 0012 Ideas climb rungs

## Decision

An idea climbs one rung at a time: idea, folded, enforced, proven. Many early ideas came from an earlier design conversation whose transcript is not kept in this repository.

## Basis

Architect.

- idea: a page in `docs/ideas/` naming its source.
- folded: in docs with its law, what it composes from, what it removes, and the test that would falsify it.
- enforced: code plus a test that bites.
- proven: measured in an unscripted run.

## Consequences

- Every idea page states its rung.
- An idea is not a requirement until it is enforced.
