# Direction and drift

## Idea

A goal is a page, and any page's recent activity can be read against it as in phase (effort is adding up), drifting (busy without direction) or out of phase (something important is unattended).

A reading is not a grade. It comes with the smallest correction, and drift that persists goes to a slower review instead of being repeated daily. Nothing of this is built.

## Composes from

A measure (a page watching itself), the [clock](clock.md), and a link to a goal page.

## Needs something new

A stated formula for "how much of the activity runs with the goal". It must be chosen and tested.

## Falsifying test

When two linked pages are made to work against each other, the reading changes before any hard rule is broken. Readings are stable enough not to nag.

## Open questions

- The formula.
- How often to read.
