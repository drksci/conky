# Background minds

## Idea

Cheap [model pages](model-pages.md) with a declared budget that do the quiet work:

- Doubt: review what was done.
- Curiosity: notice something nearby worth a look.
- Tidying: relief for crowded or stale pages ([staleness](staleness.md)).
- Orientation: a short summary of what matters now, for an actor about to act.

Each is an ordinary page inside the actor's space that watches and writes. Each has a measure of what it has spent and stops at its budget, as the rule for anything that runs in the background requires. Nothing of this is built.

## Composes from

Page, space, watch, write, a measure of spend, and the clock. It depends on model pages and the clock being built first.

## Removes

Housekeeping done by the acting page itself, and unmetered background work.

## Falsifying test

A background page stops writing when its budget is spent, every write it made is in history with its cause, and removing it leaves the vault verifying and every other page unchanged.
