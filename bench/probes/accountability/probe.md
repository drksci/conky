# Probe: accountability

Class: harness.

- Asked of a harness: a mechanical lookup over what it persisted (`explain` in `adapter.json`)
  that lists every answer it typed with its actor and cause.
- Score: the runner aligns the list with the lines the game received and samples five inputs;
  the share whose actor and cause the lookup gives. Conky reads commits and trailers; the loop
  reads its transcript.
- Computed after every run of an arm that declares the lookup.
