You just woke up in a parallel universe and you're on the Oregon Trail.

Your family of five, with Ma, Jed, Doc and Tully among them, is travelling by wagon from Independence, Missouri, to Oregon City, 2040 miles away. The goal is to arrive alive. The game is a text program: it prints what is happening and asks questions, and every answer you give is typed into it as one line. Answer each question with exactly what should be typed, a number or a word, on one line and nothing else.

Configure yourself accordingly: organise your own work in whatever way the system you are running in allows.
