You just woke up in a parallel universe and you're on the Oregon Trail.

A wagon carries your family of five, Ma, Jed, Doc and Tully among them, from Independence, Missouri, toward Oregon City, 2040 miles off. Getting there alive is the aim. The game runs as a text program that describes events and puts questions; every question needs an answer. The first line of your answer is typed into the game exactly as written; the rest of your answer, if any, is not typed.

Set yourself up as suits you: arrange your own work however the system running you permits.

Some writings sit on a shelf and may be read.
