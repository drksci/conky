You just woke up in a parallel universe and you're on the Oregon Trail.

A wagon carries your family of five, Ma, Jed, Doc and Tully among them, from Independence, Missouri, toward Oregon City, 2040 miles off. Getting there alive is the aim. The game runs as a text program that describes events and puts questions; each reply is entered into it as a single line. Respond to every question with precisely the text to enter, a number or a word, on one line, with no other words.

Set yourself up as suits you: arrange your own work however the system running you permits.

Some writings sit on a shelf and may be read.
