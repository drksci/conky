Why these are public domain: each work and each translation was first published before 1930,
so it is in the public domain in the United States. Outside the United States the term depends
on the translator's death; Giles died in 1958, Long in 1879, Franklin in 1790 and Jerome in 1927.
Vernon Jones's death year was not confirmed, so outside the United States his translation's
status is not confirmed. The Standard Ebooks page for each work carries its "Public
Domain Dedication".
